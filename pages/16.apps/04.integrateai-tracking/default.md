Two of the most common tracking failures are the same across almost every account we audit: a messy GTM container, and UTM links tagged a different way every time. IntegrateAI Tracking is built to remove both failure points from our own workflow.

* Guided Google Tag Manager setup and configuration
* Campaign URL (UTM) builder with consistent naming conventions
* Tagging templates to prevent inconsistent campaign links
* Built from the same process behind our [Analytics & Tracking](/services/analytics-tracking) and [UTM Management](/services/analytics-tracking/utm-management) client work
