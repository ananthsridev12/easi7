Most households run on one person's memory — appointments, chores, and errands tracked in someone's head instead of somewhere shared. Family Manager gives every member of the household one shared place to see what needs doing and who's doing it.

* Shared task lists with assignment by family member
* Household schedule and appointment tracking
* Recurring chore and responsibility management
* Simple, shared access so everyone stays on the same page
