Most budgeting tools are either too basic to be useful or too complex to keep up with. Personal Finance is built around the handful of things that actually matter: what came in, what went out, and whether you're on track for what you're saving toward.

* Expense tracking by category
* Budget setting and monthly tracking
* Savings goal planning and progress
* A simple, honest view of where money actually goes
