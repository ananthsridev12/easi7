## Why EASI7 exists

Most businesses don't have a marketing problem - they have a marketing-infrastructure problem. Five vendors touch five different tools, nobody owns the tracking, and every report tells a slightly different story. EASI7 was built to fix that: one accountable team running analytics, paid media, SEO, and automation as a single connected system, not five disconnected line items.

Our approach is **data infrastructure first, then execution**. Clean tracking before campaigns. Proper attribution before budget decisions. Automation before scale. It's slower in week one and faster in every week after.

## What we bring

* **Analytics &amp; Tracking** - GA4, GTM, PostHog, data layer design, attribution tracking, Looker Studio dashboards, and SQL queries. Clean data before any campaign launch.
* **Performance Marketing** - Google Search, Shopping, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads. Multi-channel campaign management optimised for qualified traffic and CPL reduction, not just impressions.
* **Technical SEO &amp; AEO** - Schema markup, structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Article), answer engine optimisation, AI-assisted keyword research, and technical audits.
* **CRM &amp; Automation** - Full CRM setup and integration, email marketing workflows, WhatsApp automation, lead nurturing sequences, and MQL-to-SQL conversion optimisation.
