## Where we are right now

EASI7 isn't running open full-time roles at the moment - we're a small, founder-led studio and we're growing deliberately rather than staffing ahead of demand.

## Who we're open to hearing from

We do work with specialist collaborators on a project basis - designers, developers, and content specialists who want occasional, well-scoped project work rather than a full-time role. If that's you, and your portfolio is strong in one of our service areas, send a short note and examples to [hello@easi7.in](mailto:hello@easi7.in).

We don't keep a generic CV inbox - a specific note about what you do and a couple of relevant links gets read faster than a formal application.
