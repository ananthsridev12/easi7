## Founder-led, by design

EASI7 is led directly by [Ananth Sridev](/about), who works every engagement rather than handing it off to a junior account team after the pitch. That's a deliberate structural choice, not a limitation we're working around - clients get direct access to the person who actually understands their account.

## How we scale

For work outside core analytics, performance marketing, and content - design, video production, or larger development builds - we bring in trusted specialists on a per-project basis, vetted and directed by the same person accountable for the strategy. You get specialist quality without losing a single point of accountability to a rotating account team.

As specific capabilities become a consistent, recurring need, they get built in-house rather than sourced project by project. [Get in touch](/contact) if you want to know exactly who'd be working on your account before starting.
