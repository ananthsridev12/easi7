## The challenge

DhanLAP, the lending platform run by Ark Neo Financial Services, was running paid campaigns with no reliable way to measure what they produced. There was no GA4 or GTM implementation, no conversion tracking, and no structured way to know whether a "lead" in the CRM was actually qualified or just a form fill.

## What we did

We started with infrastructure, not media spend. GA4 and Google Tag Manager were implemented from the ground up, with a custom data layer covering every meaningful on-site action - form starts, form completions, calculator usage, and document uploads specific to a lending funnel.

With clean tracking in place, we launched Google Ads Search campaigns targeting high-intent financial keywords - problem-aware searches and category terms rather than broad brand or generic finance terms - alongside Meta and LinkedIn Ads for awareness and retargeting.

On the SEO side, we optimised content structure for answer engines using schema markup and structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Article) to improve visibility in featured snippets and AI-generated answers.

The final layer was CRM automation: a lead-scoring model based on ICP fit and behavioural signals, feeding an automated nurture sequence so no lead sat untouched waiting for manual follow-up.

## The results

* **35% reduction in cost per lead**, driven by tighter keyword targeting and better landing page alignment
* **40% increase in qualified traffic**, measured against the newly implemented conversion events - not raw sessions
* **25% improvement in MQL-to-SQL conversion**, attributable directly to the lead-scoring and automation layer
* SQL-based dashboards in Looker Studio giving the team a single, trusted view of funnel performance

The infrastructure built for DhanLAP is still the backbone of their marketing reporting today - every new campaign gets measured against the same clean baseline.
