<p>A growing share of searches now get answered directly on the results page or inside an AI-generated summary, with no click to any website at all. It's one of the most common questions we get asked on discovery calls: "if people aren't clicking through, why does SEO still matter?"</p>

<p>Our short answer: visibility inside those zero-click answers is now part of the game, not a reason to ignore it. Structured data, direct-answer content formatting, and clear entity signals all affect whether your business gets cited inside an AI Overview or a featured snippet — see our take in <a href="/services/seo/ai-seo">AI SEO</a> and the <a href="/knowledge-hub/what-is-answer-engine-optimization">Answer Engine Optimisation glossary entry</a>.</p>
