B2B and SaaS buyers rarely decide alone, and the sales cycle is measured in months, not days. Marketing has to sustain a prospect's attention and answer a buying committee's questions, not just capture a single click.

* Demand generation and lifecycle nurture for long consideration cycles
* Account-based marketing for named target accounts
* Content built for buying-committee research, not just top-of-funnel awareness
* Sales and marketing alignment on lead scoring and handoff
* Analytics built around pipeline, not just traffic

See our [B2B Marketing & Lead Generation](/services/b2b-marketing-lead-generation) services, or the [DhanLAP case study](/case-studies/dhanlap-marketing-infrastructure) for a related build in a considered-purchase category.
