Most cold email fails on deliverability before it ever fails on copy. We fix the technical foundation first - SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and domain warming - so the message actually reaches the inbox before we worry about what it says.

* Sending domain setup and warming, separate from your primary domain
* SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configuration for inbox placement
* List building and verification to keep bounce rates low
* Sequence design: 3&ndash;5 touches with genuine value in each, not just a follow-up nudge
* Personalisation at the account and role level, not just a merge-tagged first name
* Reply-rate and positive-response tracking, not just open rates

### What we track instead of opens

Open-rate tracking is increasingly unreliable post-Apple Mail Privacy Protection. We optimise and report on reply rate and positive-response rate - the numbers that actually correlate with pipeline.
