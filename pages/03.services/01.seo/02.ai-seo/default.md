A growing share of searches never produce a click — the answer is generated directly by Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or a voice assistant. AI SEO is how you make sure your business is the source those answers point to, not the one they skip.

* Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) — structuring pages to directly answer specific questions
* Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) — optimising for citation inside AI-generated summaries
* FAQ, HowTo, and Article schema markup built for machine extraction
* Entity and structured-data clarity so AI models can verify who you are
* Content restructuring: direct answers first, supporting detail after
* Monitoring brand mentions and citations across AI search surfaces

### How this differs from classic SEO

Classic SEO optimises for a ranking position in a list of links. AI SEO optimises for being lifted out of your page entirely and placed inside someone else's answer — which means clarity, directness, and verifiable structure matter more than keyword density ever did.
