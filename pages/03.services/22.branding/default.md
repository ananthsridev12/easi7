Most rebrand conversations start with 'we need a new logo,' which is usually the wrong place to start. A logo is a downstream decision - the real work is positioning: what the business actually stands for, and why that should matter to the person deciding whether to buy.

We build in that order - strategy first, then identity and logo work tested at billboard size and favicon size alike, then guidelines documented so anyone can apply the brand correctly without guessing. The result is a brand that feels like one company across web, social, email, and print, not five slightly different ones.
