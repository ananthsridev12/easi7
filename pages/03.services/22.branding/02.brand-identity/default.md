A strong identity is a system that stays recognisable across a business card, a billboard, and a mobile app icon - not just a nice logo on its own.

* Colour palette and typography system
* Logo and mark design
* Imagery and iconography style
* Application across touchpoints
* Identity documentation and usage rules
