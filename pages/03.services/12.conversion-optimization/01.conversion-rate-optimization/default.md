Changing a button colour and calling it CRO is how most "optimisation" fails quietly. Real CRO starts with evidence, not opinion.

* Funnel and heatmap diagnostic audits
* Hypothesis-driven test prioritisation
* A/B and multivariate testing
* Statistical significance and validation
* Post-test rollout and documentation
