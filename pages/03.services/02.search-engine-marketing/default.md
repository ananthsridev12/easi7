Paid marketing spend gets wasted in predictable ways: broad targeting turned on before there's enough conversion data to support it, budget spread evenly across channels regardless of which one is actually working, and campaigns left running unreviewed for months because nobody owns the weekly optimisation.

We run paid media as an ongoing discipline, not a launch-and-leave campaign. That means structuring accounts around how buyers actually search and scroll, reviewing performance weekly rather than monthly, and moving budget toward what the data supports rather than what a stakeholder assumes is working. Google, Meta, and LinkedIn each reward different account structures and different testing cadences - we run each on its own logic instead of copy-pasting one playbook across all three.

The channels below cover the full paid mix: search intent capture, social audience-building, and the display and retargeting layers that keep a brand present between the first click and the eventual conversion.
