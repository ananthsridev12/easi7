Manual lead entry is where leads get lost, delayed, or duplicated. A proper integration means the CRM is always the accurate, current source of truth.

* Website and form-to-CRM integration
* Ad platform lead sync (Google, Meta, LinkedIn)
* Data field mapping and validation
* Duplicate prevention rules
* CRM-to-marketing-tool bidirectional sync
