A chatbot that confidently answers incorrectly does more damage to trust than no chatbot at all - which is exactly why grounding every response in real documentation, rather than general model knowledge, matters more than how natural the conversation sounds.

We scope each chatbot to what it can answer reliably. Website chatbots catch the moment a visitor has an unanswered question and would otherwise just leave. Support chatbots resolve the handful of questions that repeat constantly, freeing human time for tickets that need judgment. Lead generation chatbots qualify conversationally, capturing the same data a form would with less visitor friction. Internal knowledge assistants answer employee questions from documentation that already exists but is scattered across five different tools nobody wants to search.

Confidence thresholds and clean escalation to a human are built in from day one - not an afterthought once something goes wrong publicly.
