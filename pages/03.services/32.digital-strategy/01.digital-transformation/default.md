"Digital transformation" gets reduced to "redesign the website" more often than it should - the real work usually sits in process and tooling decisions that never show up on the front end at all, and are far more consequential to how the business actually operates.

We start with an honest current-state assessment - separating what's genuinely working from what's a workaround people have quietly gotten used to - before recommending a single tool or sequencing a single phase. That assessment becomes the basis for a roadmap ordered by dependency, with change management built in from the start rather than bolted on after a rollout meets resistance nobody anticipated.
