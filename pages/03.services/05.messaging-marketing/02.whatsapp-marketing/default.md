For a large share of Indian consumers and SMBs, WhatsApp is the primary inbox - not email. We build WhatsApp Business API automation that meets customers where they already are.

* WhatsApp Business API setup and template message approval
* Automated lead-nurturing sequences triggered by CRM events
* Order confirmations, shipping updates, and appointment reminders
* Two-way conversational flows for support and qualification
* Broadcast campaigns segmented by opt-in list, respecting Meta's messaging policies
* Integration with existing CRM and lead-scoring systems

### Where this fits into the funnel

WhatsApp automation usually sits downstream of a lead capture - a form fill or ad click hands off into a WhatsApp sequence that qualifies the lead conversationally before a salesperson ever gets involved.
