Paid campaigns built to drive qualified leads and revenue, not just impressions. Smart bidding, audience segmentation, and funnel-aligned creative across every major paid channel.

* Google Ads &mdash; Search, Display &amp; Shopping campaigns
* Meta Ads (Facebook &amp; Instagram) across the full funnel
* LinkedIn Ads for B2B lead generation and account-based targeting
* Lead generation funnel design and optimisation
* Landing page conversion optimisation for paid traffic
* CPL and ROAS improvement strategies, reviewed weekly
* Multi-channel campaign management with unified reporting

### How we run it

Cold, warm, and hot audiences get different messages and different budget allocations — not the same ad shown to everyone. We review search terms and placements weekly, build negative keyword lists aggressively, and only let Smart Bidding take over once there's enough conversion data for it to work with.
